How have the ECB's rates moved since 1999?

How to read it. The three ECB rates are step lines: each one stays flat until the ECB changes it on a decision date, then jumps. Marginal lending (top) and the deposit facility (bottom) frame a corridor around the main refinancing rate (MRO). The 3-month Euribor is a market rate, not a decision, so it is a normal line over monthly averages. Scroll to zoom, hover to compare.Drag the slider to zoom, tap the chart to compare.

Euribor is set by banks lending to each other, not by the ECB, yet it follows the decision rates: that is how an ECB decision reaches the interest rates people and companies pay.

02

What do the ECB's rates mean?

Three rates set the price of central-bank money; Euribor shows what banks actually charge each other.

Deposit facility rate
What banks earn on money they park at the ECB overnight. The floor of the corridor.
Main refinancing rate (MRO)
What banks pay to borrow from the ECB for a week against collateral. The middle of the corridor.
Marginal lending rate
What banks pay to borrow from the ECB overnight in an emergency. The ceiling of the corridor.
Euribor 3M
The rate at which banks in the euro area lend to each other for three months. A market rate, used for many loans and mortgages.
Negative rates
Below zero, banks pay to keep money at the ECB, which pushes them to lend it out instead.
Decision date
The day a new rate takes effect after an ECB Governing Council meeting. Between two decisions the rate does not change.
