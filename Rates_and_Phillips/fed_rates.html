Federal Reserve – EFFR and SOFR

EFFR (effective federal funds rate) is the overnight rate banks actually pay each other for unsecured loans, and the rate the Fed steers. SOFR (secured overnight financing rate, from Apr 2018) is the overnight rate on loans backed by Treasuries, the benchmark that replaced LIBOR. Data: FRED, last value of each week.
