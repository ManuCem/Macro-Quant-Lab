Federal Reserve – Administered Interest Rates

The Fed's equivalents of the ECB's three rates. ON RRP (overnight reverse repo, from Sep 2013) is the floor, IORB (interest on reserve balances; called IOER before Jul 2021, from Oct 2008) is the middle rate that steers the market, and the discount rate (primary credit rate, from Jan 2003) is the ceiling.
