VIX vs realized volatility

Does the VIX overprice volatility?

Yes, on average. From 1996 to 2026 the VIX sat 3.79 points above the volatility the S&P 500 actually had over the next 21 trading days. A typical miss, in either direction, was about 8.16 points, so on any given day it could be far off both ways.

02

How do we score a forecast?

Two numbers: the average gap (+3.79) and the typical miss (8.16).

VIX (implied volatility)
The market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over roughly the next month, read from option prices and quoted as an annualized %.
Realized volatility, next 21d
What volatility actually turned out to be: the standard deviation of the S&P 500's daily returns over the 21 trading days AFTER each date, the fair comparison for a forward-looking measure like the VIX. A backward rolling standard deviation would look the wrong way.
Log return
ln(today / yesterday). Used instead of the simple % change because log returns add up over time and treat gains and losses symmetrically, which is what the annualization math assumes.
Annualizing
Daily volatility x √252 (trading days in a year) x 100, so it is in the same units as the VIX.
Bias
The average gap, VIX - realized. Positive: the VIX overpriced volatility on average.
RMSE
Root mean squared error: the typical size of the gap in either direction. Big misses weigh more.

03

What did we learn?

Four takeaways.

  1. On average the VIX sat above what followed, by 3.79 points over 7,525 trading days.
  2. The misses are bigger than the average. The typical miss (RMSE 8.16) is larger than the average gap (+3.79): on a given day the VIX can be well off in either direction.
  3. The average hides the shocks. Around big sell-offs the VIX was often too low, not too high: see the companion page on four market shocks.
  4. Limits. One average over three decades mixes calm and crisis years. Realized volatility uses daily closes only, and 21 trading days is close to, but not exactly, the VIX's one-month window. The last 21 days cannot be scored yet: their future has not happened.
