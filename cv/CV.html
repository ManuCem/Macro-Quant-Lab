Macro Quant Lab · Personal research
VIX vs realized volatility

Was the VIX right in four market shocks?

  • Compares each day's VIX (the ~30-day volatility forecast) with the volatility that actually followed over 21 trading days
  • Covers the dot-com crash, 2008, Covid and the 2025 tariffs
  • Correlation ~0.7; VIX above realized volatility on ~84% of days
  • 2008 and Covid: realized volatility beat the VIX
0.7correlation
84%days VIX > realized

Conclusion: the VIX is a good gauge but a biased one. It charges a premium for insurance in calm markets and underestimates the real crashes.

Macro Quant Lab · Macro & interest rates
Interest rates & the Phillips curve

Did near-zero rates bring a recovery?

  • Monthly US data from FRED: unemployment, core inflation, Fed funds rate
  • 2008: unemployment needed 75 months to recover, rates at the floor for 85
  • Covid: recovery in 20 months, but core inflation hit 6.6%
  • Real rate fell to about −6%
75 vs 20months to recover
6.6%core inflation peak

Conclusion: cheap money bought a fast recovery after Covid, but the price was inflation. After 2008 it bought neither.

Predictive Pricing on AWS

Can messy CSVs become a pricing model, cheaply?

  • 10 messy CSV feeds cleaned with Python and regex on EC2, then stored in S3
  • Glue and Athena validate the data with SQL, scanning about 90% less on Parquet
  • SageMaker trains an XGBoost price model; EventBridge, SNS and CloudWatch run and monitor it
10CSV feeds
−90%data scanned

Conclusion: clean data and cheap storage matter more than the model.
